# Design Intelligence

Answer: **What perceptual, cognitive or behavioral mechanism may explain this specific issue?**

Use psychology to explain observed or plausible mechanisms, not to decorate recommendations with named laws.

Layers: perception/salience/grouping → attention → comprehension/mental models → memory/cognitive load → decision/friction/motivation → action/feedback → remembered experience.

Use relevant Laws of UX and Gestalt principles as evidence-informed heuristics. Avoid pseudo-precision and universal numeric claims. Distinguish requirements, broadly supported principles, heuristics and context-dependent hypotheses.

## Predictive attention
Estimate likely attention competition from hierarchy, size, contrast, position, proximity, grouping, motion and density. Call it a hypothesis/scan-path risk, never an actual heatmap or measured eye-tracking result.

## Behavioral hypothesis
Observed/problem signal → plausible mechanism → intervention → expected behavior → measurement that could falsify it. Include autonomy/ethics; avoid deceptive/coercive patterns.
