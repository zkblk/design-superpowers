# Visual Hierarchy
Hierarchy answers what is noticed first, what supports it, what is actionable and what can wait.
**DEFAULT** Coordinate position, size, weight, contrast, spacing and grouping; do not maximize every signal.
**DEFAULT** Primary actions/data dominate locally; metadata/secondary actions recede without becoming illegible.
**MUST** Semantic state cannot rely on color alone.
**AVOID** equal weight everywhere, badge overload and decoration competing with task-critical content.
