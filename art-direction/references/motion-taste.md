# Motion taste

Use motion to show state change, origin/destination, hierarchy, continuity or personality.

- Direct manipulation should feel immediate.
- Enter/exit motion should reflect spatial relationship when one exists.
- Small UI feedback is usually quicker and quieter than page/brand transitions.
- Avoid stacking unrelated transforms and easing curves.
- Springs are useful when physical continuity helps; they are not a universal premium effect.
- Stagger only when sequence improves comprehension or character; long cascades create latency.
- Preserve interruption and reversal where users can change their mind.
- Test reduced-motion behavior.
