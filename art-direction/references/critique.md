# Fresh-eye taste critique

Ask, in order:
1. Can I identify the intended visual point of view in five seconds?
2. What is visually dominant, and is it the right thing?
3. Which choices feel intentional versus inherited/default?
4. Is there one coherent grammar for type, spacing, shape, color, depth, imagery and motion?
5. Where has componentization turned into cardification or box proliferation?
6. Is brand expression appropriate to the surface mode?
7. What feels generic enough that another unrelated AI-generated product could look the same?
8. Which three changes would produce the largest improvement?
9. Did expressive choices harm comprehension, accessibility, performance or interaction predictability?

Fix high-leverage issues first. Do not chase microscopic novelty.
