# Search
**DEFAULT** Use when users know or can approximate what they seek. Keep a visible label/clear affordance.
**CONTEXTUAL** Choose submit, instant, scoped or autocomplete behavior from dataset, latency and task.
**DEFAULT** Keep query visible on results and make clear/refine easy; expose scope when ambiguous.
**MUST** Distinguish no-results from loading/error.
**AVOID** search as a substitute for broken IA, uncontrolled remote requests, or icon-only search where discoverability matters.
