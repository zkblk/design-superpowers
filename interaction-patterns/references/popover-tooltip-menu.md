# Popover / Tooltip / Menu
Choose by intent, not appearance.

- Tooltip: brief supplemental explanation; not required information or primary interaction.
- Menu: set of actions/choices with menu-like keyboard behavior where appropriate.
- Popover: contextual interactive surface that may contain richer controls/content.

Define trigger, placement/collision behavior, pointer + keyboard access, focus policy, dismissal, layering, relationship to trigger, touch behavior, content limits, reduced motion and accessible semantics. Never rely on hover alone for required information. Ensure overlays remain visible within viewport/container constraints and do not sit under surrounding layers.
