# Scatter and Distribution
## Scatter
Use for relationship between two quantitative variables. Axes/units explicit. Add trend only when analytically justified; correlation does not imply causation.
## Distribution
Histogram shows frequency across quantitative bins; box/dot/other distribution views fit spread/outliers/comparison when audience can interpret them.
**DEFAULT** Choose bins/scales deliberately.
**AVOID** line charts for unordered observations, undisclosed story-changing binning and decorative smoothing implying unsupported precision.
