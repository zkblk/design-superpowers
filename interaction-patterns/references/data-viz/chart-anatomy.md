# Chart Anatomy
Potential anatomy: descriptive title, plot/frame, axes, ticks, units, series encoding, legend/direct labels, annotations/reference lines, tooltip/details, controls.
**DEFAULT** Prefer direct labels when they reduce legend lookup. Make units/scales clear.
**MUST** Encodings must not misrepresent magnitude; color cannot be the sole essential carrier.
**CONTEXTUAL** Tooltips supplement; essential understanding must not exist only on hover.
**AVOID** chartjunk, redundant legends, unexplained abbreviations, misleading axis treatment and decoration competing with data.
